brain_zero | X1 Carbon | Smooth lids with 2D inlay artwork

Use the base STL, lid STL, and SVG from the same folder. Version A has
a recessed jack. Version B has a flush jack. Print the base floor down
in the lid's main colour.
The lid STL has a flat outside face with four vent slots and is supplied
face down. The SVG has the logo and INVERTER label as filled outlines
in millimetres.

Place the SVG in Bambu Studio
1. View the smooth exterior face with the socket edge at the left,
   as shown in placement-preview.png. Temporarily turn the lid face up
   if needed to place the art on its exterior.
2. Add the matching SVG to the lid as a Modifier and assign the second
   PETG filament. Keep the artwork at the size below. If the slicer
   centres on the ink and discards the blank SVG margins, position the
   ink bounds using the margins or centre below. Measurements are from
   the lid's rectangular bounds in the exterior view, socket at left.
3. Place the modifier from the outside surface to 0.40 mm into the lid.
   Put the lid and modifier back face down together. Slice for the
   X1 Carbon with your PETG and two-colour setup, then check the filament
   preview. With a 0.16 mm first layer and 0.12 mm regular layers, the
   artwork should appear on the first three layers.
