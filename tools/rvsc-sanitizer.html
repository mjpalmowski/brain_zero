Prepare a compatibility profile

Convert a current VEConfigure .rvsc backup into a shareable compatibility.json. The conversion runs in your browser's memory. No file is uploaded or sent over the network.

Run locally

  1. Save this complete rvsc-sanitizer.html file to your computer and open it in your browser.
  2. Choose your original .rvsc backup below. Keep the original on your computer.
  3. Save compatibility.json, then attach that file to your brain_zero compatibility issue.

The output includes the inverter model code, firmware and setting descriptors. It leaves out the original serial number, unit ID, authorization data, timestamps, saved settings and Assistant contents. The original filename is not copied.

Choose a backup to prepare a sanitized profile.

Supports backups for one standalone 230 V MultiPlus-II. A local .vsc save is a different format; renaming it does not convert it. This tool reads the backup and does not change your inverter.
