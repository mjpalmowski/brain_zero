  1. Open your controller’s System → Inverter settings page.
  2. Choose Submit to developer. Your compatibility profile appears here for review.
  3. Enter your inverter model and submit.

On an older build, choose Download compatibility profile and select the downloaded compatibility.json below.

We aim to verify new model and firmware combinations within 48 hours of a valid submission.
